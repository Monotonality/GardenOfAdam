# Housing Affordability — Rent vs. Buy

- **Org / context:** Independent research
- **Dates:** 2026 – Present
- **Role:** Lead Researcher
- **Status:** Building
- **Preview:** Quantifying rent-vs-own breakevens across U.S. metros, 2000–2024.

## Summary

Quantifying how the post-pandemic surge in home prices and mortgage rates shifted the
breakeven between owning and renting across U.S. metros, 2000–2024.

## Research approach

- Analyzing FMR, FHFA HPI, 30-year fixed mortgage rates, median income, and CPI rent across thousands of counties.
- Identifying metros with the greatest affordability deterioration and price-to-rent shifts.
- Producing a paper and presentation for homebuyers, REIT investors, and housing policy boards.

## Deliverables

- Research paper + presentation (in progress).
- Price-to-rent ratio graphs per metro (placeholders to be added to the final report).
